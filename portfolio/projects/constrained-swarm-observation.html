Constrained Swarm Observation

2026.09

Overview

How do flight constraints change what a drone swarm can observe of a known moving target in confined geometry? Varies seven constraint axes across corridor and corner maps, separating feasibility, static observation quality (bearing-only CRLB), and what survives closed-loop flight.

Technologies

Python Robotics Simulation SLSQP Optimization ORCA Safety Filter CRLB Analysis

Features

  • Largest Static Effects - Standoff 2 → 3.5 m +2.03 cm, gimbal → fixed 20° nadir +1.96 cm, 8 → 4 drones +1.09 cm (baseline CRLB 2.63 cm).
  • Grid-Certified Feasibility - Every input checked before solving; single-axis cells stayed feasible while pairs (narrow cone + large standoff / low ceiling) removed the feasible region.
  • Flight Costs More - Nadir 30° raised flown CRLB +4.40 cm vs +1.27 cm statically; safety held in 104/108 episodes, with 3 standoff episodes showing degenerate observations.
  • Estimator Check - Maximum-likelihood estimator reached the bound under the same noise model (median RMSE/CRLB 0.999).
